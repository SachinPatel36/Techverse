Holidays & Special Situations

Non-Delivery Days

We do not deliver on the following days:

  • National holidays (Republic Day, Independence Day, Gandhi Jayanti)
  • Major festivals (Diwali, Holi, Eid, Christmas)
  • Sundays (in select locations)

Peak Season Delays

During festive seasons and sale periods, delivery times may be extended by 1-2 days due to high order volumes. We'll keep you informed of any delays.

Weather-Related Delays

In case of severe weather conditions, natural disasters, or unforeseen circumstances, deliveries may be delayed. Customer safety is our priority.

Shipping FAQs

Can I change my delivery address after placing an order?

Yes, you can change the delivery address before the order is shipped. Contact our customer support immediately with your order ID and new address. Once shipped, address changes are not possible.

What if I'm not available during delivery?

Our delivery partner will attempt delivery 3 times. If unsuccessful, you can collect the package from the nearest courier office or request re-delivery at a convenient time.

Do you ship to P.O. Boxes?

Unfortunately, we do not ship to P.O. Box addresses. Please provide a complete residential or commercial address for delivery.

Can I expedite my order after it's placed?

Contact our support team within 2 hours of placing your order. We'll try to upgrade your shipping if the order hasn't been processed yet (additional charges may apply).

What happens if my package is damaged?

Do not accept damaged packages. Take photos and refuse delivery. Contact us immediately, and we'll arrange for a replacement at no extra cost.

Do you offer international shipping?

Currently, we only ship within India. International shipping will be available soon. Subscribe to our newsletter for updates.
