Returns & Refunds

Easy returns with our 30-day money back guarantee

Our Return Policy

At Techverse, we want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If you're not happy with your product, we offer a hassle-free 30-day return policy on most items.

Eligible Items

  • Laptops and computers
  • Smartphones and tablets
  • Audio equipment (headphones, earbuds, speakers)
  • Accessories (keyboards, mice, hubs, cables)
  • All other tech products sold on our platform

Return Conditions

  • Product must be in original condition with all accessories
  • Original packaging must be intact and undamaged
  • All tags, labels, and protective films must be present
  • No physical damage or signs of use
  • Return initiated within 30 days of delivery

30-Day Money Back Guarantee

Return any product within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, no questions asked. We believe in the quality of our products and stand behind every purchase you make.

Refund Process

Once we receive your returned item, we'll inspect it and process your refund quickly and efficiently.

Timeline

  • Inspection: 2-3 business days after receiving the item
  • Refund initiation: 1-2 business days after approval
  • Credit to your account: 5-7 business days depending on your bank

Refund Method

Refunds will be issued to the original payment method used for the purchase.

  • Credit/Debit Card: Refund to the same card
  • UPI/Net Banking: Refund to your bank account
  • Cash on Delivery: Bank transfer refund

Non-Returnable Items

For hygiene and safety reasons, the following items cannot be returned:

  • Opened software or digital products
  • Products with tampered serial numbers
  • Items marked as final sale or clearance
  • Gift cards and vouchers
  • Downloaded software or digital content

Exchanges

We currently don't offer direct exchanges. If you need a different product, please return the original item for a refund and place a new order for the item you want.
